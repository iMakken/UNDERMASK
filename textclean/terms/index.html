UNDERMASK Text Clean is a small utility. It removes hidden characters and formatting leftovers from text, on your phone. That is all it promises.

1. What this app is

Text Clean removes invisible characters, non-standard spaces, extra whitespace and, if you choose, formatting such as Markdown from text you paste into it. All processing happens on your device.

2. What it does not do

Text Clean does not rewrite your text and does not remove statistical watermarks that some AI models build into their choice of words. It makes no promise about how any AI detector, teacher, employer, or platform will treat the cleaned text.

You are responsible for how you use the text, including any rules on AI-generated content that apply at your school, workplace, or on the platforms where you post it.

3. Results

The app is designed to remove only characters you cannot see and formatting you choose to remove. Still, please read the cleaned text before you rely on it. In some writing systems invisible joining characters carry meaning, and removing them can change how words are displayed. The original stays in the input field until you clear it.

4. Free use

The free version allows 5 cleans a day for texts of up to 10,000 characters. Cleaning text that is already clean does not use up a free clean. The count is kept on your device and resets daily.
