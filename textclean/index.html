TEXT CLEAN

Clean what you can't see.

AI chats leave invisible characters and formatting leftovers in the text you copy. Text Clean finds them and removes them in one tap — right on your phone.

How it works

01

Paste. The app instantly shows how many hidden characters it found.

02

Clean. One tap removes them and shows exactly what was fixed.

03

Use it. Copy, share to any messenger, or save as a file.

What it removes

  • Invisible characters — zero-width spaces and joiners, word joiners, byte order marks, soft hyphens.
  • Hidden tag characters that can carry text you cannot see.
  • Text direction controls that change how text is displayed.
  • Non-standard spaces — non-breaking and narrow spaces become normal ones.
  • Extra whitespace — trailing spaces and long runs of empty lines.

Emoji stay intact, including family emoji and flags. Text Clean does not rewrite your words.
