What it fixes

  • Weird gaps and spacing that show up when you paste text into a post, an email or a document.
  • Invisible characters that sneak in when you copy from AI chats and travel with your text wherever it goes.
  • Text that misbehaves — breaks a layout, a search or a form for no visible reason.
  • AI formatting leftovers like **asterisks**, # headings and curly quotes (Pro).

Your words stay exactly as they are. Emoji too.

The technical list
  • Invisible characters — zero-width spaces and joiners, word joiners, byte order marks, soft hyphens.
  • Hidden tag characters that can carry text you cannot see.
  • Text direction controls that change how text is displayed.
  • Non-standard spaces — non-breaking and narrow spaces become normal ones.
  • Extra whitespace — trailing spaces and long runs of empty lines.
