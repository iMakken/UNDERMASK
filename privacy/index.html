Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026
Applies to the UNDERMASK mobile application and the website undermask.app
UNDERMASK is developed by Individual Entrepreneur Andrei Akentev, operating as NNCNC Lab.

UNDERMASK has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We never learn who you are — not because we choose not to look, but because we never ask and operate no system capable of connecting an answer to a person.

1. What we never collect

We do not ask for and never receive:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your age, gender, city, country, or any other demographic information.
  • Any login, social account, or contact list.
  • Any advertising identifier or device fingerprint.

There is no registration and no sign-in. The application opens and works immediately.

2. The anonymous device identifier

When you first open the app, it generates a random identifier — a string of characters with no meaning, created on your device. It contains no information about you or your phone.

Its only purpose is to prevent the same device from answering the same question more than once, which is what keeps the statistics meaningful. It tells our server "same device or different device" — never "who".

3. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your Drift Level and the number of questions you have answered.
  • Which questions you have already seen, so they are not shown again.
  • Which option you selected for each question.
  • Your app settings.

Your personal answer history exists only on your device. We cannot read it, retrieve it, or restore it.

4. What is stored on our server

Our server holds the questions themselves and anonymous counters — how many people in total selected each option. When you answer, one number goes up by one.

We deliberately do not store a record connecting your identifier to the option you chose. Such a record does not exist anywhere in our system. We know that 6,412 people chose a particular answer; we have no way of knowing which devices they were.

5. IP addresses and logs

Like any internet service, our server receives a connection from your device. We do not log or store IP addresses. Our hosting provider may process connection data transiently for basic infrastructure protection, but we neither retain it nor use it to identify anyone.

6. No third-party services

We use no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising networks, and no third-party trackers. No SDK inside the app reports your behaviour to anyone. This is unusual, and it is deliberate: an application built on anonymity should not quietly hand your activity to someone else.

It also means we cannot see how you use the app. If something breaks, we will only know if you tell us.

7. Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or Google Backup enabled, a copy of the app's data may be stored in your own cloud account, under Apple's or Google's terms, and restored if you reinstall or change your phone. This is a feature of your device, not of our service. We have no access to that backup and cannot read it.

8. Purchases

If paid features are offered in the future, payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details, billing address, or name. We only receive confirmation from the store that a purchase is valid.

9. Children

UNDERMASK is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, we hold no personal data belonging to children either.

10. Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. In our case these rights have an unusual answer: we hold no personal data about you to access, correct, or delete.

You are always in full control of what does exist:

  • Reset inside the app clears your Drift Level and starts your count from zero.
  • Deleting the app removes your local data from the device. If a cloud backup exists in your Apple or Google account, you can delete it through your device settings.

Note that answers already counted remain part of the anonymous totals. They are not personal data — they cannot be traced back to you, and there is nothing in them to identify.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. If a change ever affects what we collect, we will say so plainly rather than quietly editing the text.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@undermask.app

Data controller: Individual Entrepreneur Andrei Akentev (NNCNC Lab).

In short: the app knows what a crowd of anonymous devices answered. It does not know who you are, and it never did.
